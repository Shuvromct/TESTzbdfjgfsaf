Tables, widths and broken words — two files
===========================================

  public/app.js       replaces the existing file
  public/app.css      replaces the existing file

Nothing else changes. No SQL, no config. If you have not yet uploaded the
seven-file update, use these two in place of its app.js and app.css and send
the whole set together.

ONE CAUSE BEHIND MOST OF IT
Every table cell carried a rule that let a word break at any point. That rule
also tells the browser a column may be one character wide, and a table shares
out its width by those minimums — so the browser handed everything to the
longest prose column and squeezed the rest. The squeezed columns then had
nowhere to put their text, so words broke between letters.

Measured across 14 pages at 6 widths:
  ordinary words broken mid-word   328  ->  0
  tables with no header row         4   ->  0
  horizontal overflow               0   ->  0
  squeezed controls                 0   ->  0

WHAT CHANGED
  - Six kinds of column, each sized by what it holds. Identifiers, dates,
    figures, statuses and buttons size to their content and never wrap. Only
    the prose column takes the remaining width, and it is capped so one long
    description cannot starve the rest.
  - CL25-26 is one word. So is every other reference.
  - A heading sits over its own figures. The numeric headings were left
    aligned while their values were right aligned, which is why ITEMS and its
    counts sat at opposite ends of the row.
  - A table of names and counts no longer stretches to fill the page; it
    sizes to its content. The departments table went from 1270px to 529px.
  - The page grows to 1560px instead of stopping at 1180px. That was 508px of
    a 22-inch monitor doing nothing.
  - Approvals and the Log now have header rows. So do the two decision lists.
  - The matrix writes its department names upright. They were set at an angle
    and ran off the top of the header, cut mid-word.
  - Sort and Group stay together when the filter bar wraps, and the saved
    views sit inside the filter card instead of below it.

STILL THERE, ON PURPOSE
  - Hyphenated compounds still wrap at their hyphen ("student-led"), and long
    email addresses wrap on a narrow screen. Both are correct.
  - At 320px, three pages break one word by a single letter in the stacked
    phone layout. Cosmetic, and only at the narrowest width.

THE TIMELINE IS NOT IN THIS
92% of every row on it is empty: 30 weeks drawn, 2.4 weeks of bar per row,
and no dates on the bars. That needs decisions from you, not a CSS fix.
